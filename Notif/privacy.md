# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-09_

Notif does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All reminders you create are stored locally on your device, inside iOS's own notification system, and never leave it. Notif has no account, no server, and does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party services. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or lose.
