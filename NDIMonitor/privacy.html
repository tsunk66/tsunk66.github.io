NDI Monitor

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

NDI Monitor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party services. The only network activity it performs is discovering and receiving NDI® video streams on your own local network.

Local network access

NDI Monitor requests local network access (and uses Bonjour/mDNS service discovery for _ndi._tcp.) solely to find and connect to NDI sources on the same Wi‑Fi or LAN as your device. This access is never used to reach the internet, and no information about your network or the sources on it is sent anywhere outside your device.

Video data

Video received from an NDI source is decoded and displayed entirely on your device. It is never recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere by NDI Monitor.

Data collection and analytics

NDI Monitor does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking frameworks, first‑party or third‑party. No usage data, device identifiers, or diagnostic information is collected or transmitted anywhere.

Accounts and purchases

NDI Monitor does not require an account, does not use in‑app purchases, and does not show ads. Every feature is available for free.

Third‑party services

NDI Monitor does not integrate with any third‑party SDKs or services. It links Vizrt NDI AB's NDI® SDK to implement source discovery and video receive, which runs entirely on‑device.

Children's privacy

Because NDI Monitor collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that uses data differently — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
