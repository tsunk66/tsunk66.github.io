NDI CameraIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

NDI CameraIO does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party services. The only network activity it performs is sending and receiving NDI® video/audio streams on your own local network.

Camera and microphone access

NDI CameraIO requests camera and microphone access solely to capture your device's video and audio and send them out as a Full NDI stream. Camera and microphone data is processed on‑device and sent only over your local network — it is never uploaded to the developer or any third party, and nothing is recorded or saved by the app itself.

Local network access

NDI CameraIO requests local network access (and uses Bonjour/mDNS service discovery for _ndi._tcp.) solely to advertise its own NDI stream and to find and connect to NDI sources on the same Wi‑Fi or LAN as your device. This access is never used to reach the internet, and no information about your network is sent anywhere outside your device.

Video and audio data

Video and audio sent from NDI CameraIO, and video received from NDI sources in Monitor mode, stay entirely within your local network. Nothing is recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere by the app beyond that local NDI connection.

Data collection and analytics

NDI CameraIO does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking frameworks, first‑party or third‑party. No usage data, device identifiers, or diagnostic information is collected or transmitted anywhere.

Accounts and purchases

NDI CameraIO does not require an account. Any in‑app purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store; NDI CameraIO itself never sees or stores your payment information.

Third‑party services

NDI CameraIO does not integrate with any third‑party SDKs or services. It links Vizrt NDI AB's NDI® SDK to implement stream sending, discovery, and receiving, which runs entirely on‑device.

Children's privacy

Because NDI CameraIO collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that uses data differently — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
